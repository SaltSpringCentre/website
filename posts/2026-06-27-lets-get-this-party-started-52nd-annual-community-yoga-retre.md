Dearest Friend,

Once a year, our community gathers at the Centre for five days of practice, kirtan, satsang, and simply being together and we wanted YOU to be among the first to know the dates.

This year's[ 52nd Annual Community Yoga Retreat (ACYR) r](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/)uns **Thursday, July 30 through Monday, August 3, 2026**, right here at the Salt Spring Centre.

If you've been before, you know what this means: morning asana on the mound, pranayama and meditation, yoga philosophy sessions, kirtan, dance, open mics and satsang in the evenings, vegetarian meals through out the day cooked with real care, and a few days where the outside world gets quieter than usual and the vibrance of inner nature emerges to life.

If you haven't been before, you're welcome here too. ACYR is family-friendly, built for seasoned practitioners and first-timers alike.

The retreat is rooted in the teachings of Baba Hari Dass "Babaji", as generations of students have called him. It was Babaji who first asked a small group of students to start a satsang (a gathering of truth seekers) in Vancouver in the early 1970s. That weekly gathering grew into a yearly retreat, and that yearly retreat is what eventually led the community to this land , the Salt Spring Centre of Yoga was founded here in 1981. This year marks the 52nd time this community has gathered this way.

This year's theme is** " *faith is the ground for yoga to grow***** " **you'll see it on the [commemorative t-shirt](https://clients.pietsuess.com/sscy/acyr52/) we're offering this year (more on that below), but really it's just naming what's already true about ACYR: showing up, year after year, is itself an act of faith, and it's what keeps this community growing.

A few practical notes while you're marking your calendar:

- Accommodation options include **Adults Only** indoor rooms, **Camping** (for adults or families), and **Commuter/ Day Passes** if you'd rather come for a day at a time.

- **Volunteer positions** are available if you'd like to help make the retreat happen and ease the cost of attending. Contact [acyr@saltspringcentre.com](mailto:acyr@saltspringcentre.com)

- **Financial assistance is available.** If cost is a barrier, please just write to us at [acyr@saltspringcentre.com](mailto:acyr@saltspringcentre.com),

- This year's **commemorative t-shirt** is up for pre-order till July 16— details just below.

Registration is open now, and spots especially camping and indoor rooms tend to fill quickly. If you already know you're coming, this is the easiest time to register.
