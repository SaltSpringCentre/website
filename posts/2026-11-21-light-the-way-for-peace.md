# Join us for our fundraising events!

Our 2026 season has been a big and special one - and we are ready to celebrate it with you!

**We're hosting a Fundraising Weekend!**

**November 27–28, 2026 - **Enjoy Music for Peace on Friday and Gala for Peace on Saturday. Come for one evening, both events, or make it a weekend stay.

- **The full weekend: concert, gala and overnight stay.** Attend both events and stay at the Centre.

- **Music for Peace: An Evening with Sharanjeet Singh Mand.** Friday, November 27 · 7:00 p.m. · $40

- **Gala for Peace: An Evening of Connection and Celebration.** Saturday, November 28 · Mocktails and silent auction 6:00 p.m. · Dinner 7:00 p.m. · Sliding Scale, $34 · $54 · $108

If you can't come to the fundraiser events in person, we would appreciate any and all support you can offer to the Light the Way fundraiser campaign. We hope to raise $20,000 by December 31 to care for the Centre and sustain the teachings, land, and community that bring us together.

Whether you join the celebrations or make a gift from afar, we’re grateful to have you as part of this community.
