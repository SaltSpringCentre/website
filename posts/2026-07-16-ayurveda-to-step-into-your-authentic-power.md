# Ayurveda to step into your authentic power

## The journey of coming home to your highest Self begins here

[Take the quiz](https://saltspringcentre.com/discover-your-ayurveda-constitution/)

What's holding you back from the highest version of yourSelf? Sometimes the answer lies in your nature.

Ayurveda, the science of life, offers a powerful lens for understanding our unique constitution type (our Prakriti, often referred to as dosha) and how to work with our tendencies rather than against them.

[
![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/ded77705-cf5b-c766-19e6-7d0d13c467ab.jpg)
](https://saltspringcentre.com/discover-your-ayurveda-constitution/)

Your first step? **Get to know your dosha**. This simple yet powerful quiz helps you see how your inner nature might be out of balance, and how to begin realigning with the highest version of yourSelf.
