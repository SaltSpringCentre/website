Dear friends,

The long-awaited yearly event of ACYR (Annual Community Yoga Retreat) has come and gone. Impossible without your attendance and support, be it as a volunteer or guest. Our joining together creates the memories that carry us through the year with Babaji's Grace.

This year's theme, Faith.

*"…faith is the ground for Yoga to grow."*

Also from Babaji: *"What is faith? When our mind removes doubts and accepts the idea — that is faith. Faith is important because it builds up inner strength, it gives hope and helps us to stick to our path."*

Such a wonderful time of reunions and of meeting new friends, of practice and play, of waking up in Nature to magical guitars, voices and bells, Bajarangi Hanuman, morning and afternoon Arati and Yoga classes and practices, sharing Chai at Latte Da, finding treasures at Aparigraha, visiting and sharing stories with one another, the kids and teens, the Kids Program and Games, the extraordinary Ramayana play and costumes, the sweet Drum and Movement Circle, Satsang and Kirtan.

Meal circles for amazing brunch and dinner times, cheers to the cooks and the selfless surfers on the dish crew. A time of simplicity in Nature, the abundance and beauty of the farm garden, the beautiful temples and enchanting forest trails. Just sitting under Babaji's maple tree, or on the swing. The morning ceremony for Universal Peace, with even a beautiful wedding celebration.

Always a time outside of time with memories that carry us through the year and beyond. Thank you for your presence, your engagement and for joining Babaji's Centre and Satsang, ever growing family and friends.

We've put together photos from the retreat for you to relive and share:

[VIEW PHOTOS ](https://photos.app.goo.gl/sdsVrRim5pRPA6V9A)

Wishing you all a wonderful year, and as Babaji would write:

*"Wish you happy and healthy."*

*"Do your sadhana every day and be happy."*

Love and Gratitude from the ACYR Team
