This is the last email we'll send about the [Yoga Study & Service Immersion.](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/yoga-study-and-service-immersion-program/)

The application deadline is **June 15** , three days from now.

We know these decisions aren't always simple. A month away. The cost. The unknown of stepping into community. We understand. And we also know this: that quiet pull you've been feeling deserves to be taken seriously.

## Babaji wrote:

*"To serve others with no selfish motive is sacrifice. To give what others need with no strings attached is charity. To live a disciplined life is austerity. Sacrifice, charity and austerity together in action is called karma yoga."*

This program is a rare chance to live those words, not just read them.

If it's a yes: [**[Apply here]** ](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/yoga-study-and-service-immersion-program/)before June 15.

If the timing isn't right: join our waitlist and we'll reach out when the next YSSI opens.

Either way, we're glad you've been with us.

[Apply Now!](https://saltspringcentre.secure.retreat.guru/program/residential-yoga-study-and-service-immersion/?form=1&lang=en)

If you have any questions or need more information, feel free to contact us at info@saltspringcentre.com, and we’ll be happy to assist.
