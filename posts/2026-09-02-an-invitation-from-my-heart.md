Dear Satsang family,

It was great to see many of your familiar faces at ACYR once again this year and connect through our shared laughter, practice and fun.

I would like to invite you to take a look at the next retreat that I am offering at the centre. The Nature and Nurture Retreat will follow a more traditional format of yoga along with mantra chanting, group chanting practice as well as asana, pranayama, meditation and satsang on various topics of relevance to us and their scriptural insights.

It will be an intense and invigorating immersion with lots of rest in between as well. If you would like to delve into something deeper in the safe and beautiful environment of our centre, please check it out and please pass it onto anyone you think would be interested: [https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/nature-and-nurture-retreat-heal-through-yoga/](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/nature-and-nurture-retreat-heal-through-yoga/)

It is taking place October 2-4 at the Saltspring Centre.

Looking forward to connecting with you.

Ishwarya Chaitanya
