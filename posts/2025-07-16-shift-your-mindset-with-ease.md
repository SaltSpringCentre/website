## **🧠 Shift Your Mindset With Ease**

**Did you know you could heal yourself just with your mind? Did you know your beliefs are the only thing keeping you from anything you truly desire?**

In yoga, we have a bag of tricks to help the mind work with us rather than against us, and we are going to take this to the next level. Here’s how the dosha manifests in your thoughts, emotions, and beliefs, and a 1-minute tip to restore balance:

[![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/4fed8f34-63d5-3130-888e-a1b3416c5713.png)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIQHQoXtsJA)

## 🌀 **Vata (Air)**

**Stuck Emotions:**

Anxiety, confusion, indecision, fear, worry, living in the future.

**Limiting Beliefs:**
“I have to do everything myself,”
“It hasn’t worked before,”
“I can’t put myself out there.”

➡️ [**One-minute tip to balance Vata**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIQHQoXtsJA)

[![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/829404ec-0b72-fc57-da22-d59143860160.png)](https://youtube.com/shorts/g-wjN5UrzYE?feature=share)

## 🔥 **Pitta (Fire)**

**Stuck Emotions: **

Frustration, resentment, anger, hatred, jealousy, need for control**
**

**Limiting Beliefs: **

“I’m not good enough,”
”I’m a burden,”
“Others are just lucky.”

➡️ [**One-minute tip to balance Pitta**](https://youtube.com/shorts/g-wjN5UrzYE?feature=share)

[![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/02e60802-b131-02cd-4923-36c36c7e1c3e.png)](https://youtube.com/shorts/sJI_CnOSkjE?feature=share)

## 🌿 **Kapha (Earth)**

**Stuck Emotions: **

Depression, lethargy, loneliness, unmotivated, hopeless, living in the past

**Limiting Beliefs:**

*“Money is hard to make,”*

*“ finding a partner these days is impossible,”
”It’s too late to change my life now.”

*➡️ [**One-minute tip to balance Kapha**](https://youtube.com/shorts/sJI_CnOSkjE?feature=share)
