# Nature and Nurture Yoga Retreat

### 📆 October 2 to 4, 2026

Come with a Friend 🤗

## An invitation to return to rhythm, presence and nature.

This October, we're offering a weekend retreat designed to help you slow down, reconnect with yourself, and be held by both nature and tradition. The **Nature and Nurture Yoga Retreat**, led by **[Ishwarya Chaitanya,](https://saltspringcentre.secure.retreat.guru/teacher/ishwarya-chaitanya/?lang=en) **is a quiet, nourishing pause in the midst of busy lives.

[

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/96ec30a8-19b0-c7db-69b0-4e7fd74ac84a.jpg)

](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/going-deeper-silent-retreat/)

Take a pause from the pace of daily life and step into a weekend of grounding, reflection, and renewal.

Join us at the Salt Spring Centre of Yoga for the Nature & Nurture Yoga Retreat, a nourishing four-day retreat led by Ishwarya Chaitanya, designed to support rest, reconnection, and gentle transformation.

This retreat is open to both beginners and experienced practitioners, offering a safe and welcoming space to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and be held by nature.

You’ll experience:

-

Grounding yoga and meditation practices

-

Time immersed in forest and meadow landscapes

-

Nourishing vegetarian meals

-

Space for rest, reflection, and integration

-

A supportive, heart-centred community

When we slow down and listen, nature becomes our teacher.
