# Spirit of the Village

This July brings three chances to gather with us.

July 11 - [Spirit of the Village](https://saltspringcentre.com/events/spirit-of-the-village/), an evening of yoga with[ Vishvaji](https://akhandayoga.com/yogrishi-vishvketu) , song and dance that we're opening to the whole community and our Island neighbours.

July 28 - [Guru Purnima](https://saltspringcentre.com/events/guru-purnima-celebration-2026/), our Vedic yajna honouring Babaji and all spiritual teachers.

July 30 - [The 52nd Annual Community Yoga Retreat](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/)

Each one builds toward the next, and we hope you'll join us for all three.

## [*SPIRIT OF THE VILLAGE, SATURDAY, JULY 11*](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/spirit-of-the-village-tickets-1992153116599)

Last month's Raise the Roof fundraiser brought out such warmth and such a crowd that we knew it couldn't be a one-time thing. So we're calling this one Spirit of the Village, and we mean it as an offering - a full evening of food, music, and dance to open the summer together before the ACYR retreat begins.

[**Spirit of the Village**](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/spirit-of-the-village-tickets-1992153116599)

**Saturday, July 11 | 4:30–9:30pm | Salt Spring Centre of Yoga**

- 4:30pm — Asana class with [Dr.Yogrishi Vishvketu ](https://akhandayoga.com/yogrishi-vishvketu)

- 6:00pm — Vegetarian dinner

- 7:00pm — Song circle and cacao ceremony

- 8:00pm — Ecstatic dance with Syd Del

- Throughout the evening, art gallery, tarot readings, henna tattoo station, and more

Come for the full evening or choose an offering of your own. Kids and youth 16 and under are welcome at $15 for dinner, song circle, and dance. Please purchase in advance so the kitchen knows how many we're cooking for space and food are both limited.

[Get your Tickets 💖](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/spirit-of-the-village-tickets-1992153116599)

## [**GURU PURNIMA**](https://saltspringcentre.com/events/guru-purnima-celebration-2026/)

**Tuesday, July 28

8:30am–12:00pm,

lunch by donation to follow**

Two days before the ACYR retreat begins, we pause to honour Babaji and all spiritual teachers through the ancient Vedic yajna, a quiet threshold before the fuller gathering that follows.

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/92527327-5b62-4835-2f2e-ebd466268fa8.jpg)

The morning opens with an invocation to Ganesh, moves through repetitions of the Hanuman Chalisa, and closes with the Guru Purnima yajna itself, prayers, mantra, and offerings from the heart. Lunch follows by donation; please RSVP to the office. Interested in offering at the yajna, or staying the night before? Contact Mahavir or the office.

[**Learn More**](https://saltspringcentre.com/events/guru-purnima-celebration-2026/)

## [**ANNUAL COMMUNITY YOGA RETREAT**](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/)

**July 30 - August 3 **

For over fifty years, this retreat has been the heartbeat of our summer, the moment when alumni, longtime Satsang, and first-time guests all find themselves on the same land, in the same practice, at the same table. This year marks the 52nd, and the invitation is the same as it has always been: come deepen your practice, be with your people, and let the land do what it does.

Daily asana, pranayama, and meditation for all levels, kirtan and ceremony rooted in the teachings of Baba Hari Dass. Workshops, community gatherings, and wholesome vegetarian meals, with programming for kids and families throughout.
