# You’re Invited

Let's Light the Way, Together!

[

As the year draws to a close, we warmly invite you to gather with us to celebrate the community, connections, and shared experiences that make the Centre special.

**Join us for Light the Way, a Fundraising Weekend**

**November 27–28, 2026 · Salt Spring Centre of Yoga**

Enjoy Music for Peace on Friday and Gala for Peace on Saturday. Come for one evening, both events, or make it a weekend stay.

Through our Light the Way fundraiser campaign, we hope to raise $20,000 by December 31 to care for the Centre and sustain the teachings, land, and community that bring us together.

Whether you join the celebrations or make a gift from afar, we’re grateful to have you as part of this community.

[DONATE OR REGISTER HERE](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/light-the-way-a-fundraiser-weekend/)

# Bringing in the Light ✨

[

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/af73b16c-bf81-1b5b-7b64-8d115f1dc200.png)
](https://saltspringcentre.com/bringing-in-the-light-exploring-trataka/)

As the days grow shorter, we're turning our attention to light.

This month, we're exploring Trataka, a traditional yogic cleansing practice that uses steady gazing to help develop concentration and calm the mind.

From candlelight to the full moon, discover different ways to practise, along with teachings from Baba Hari Dass’ Ashtanga Yoga Primer.

[LEARN MORE](https://saltspringcentre.com/bringing-in-the-light-exploring-trataka/)

# Around the Centre

After Satsang last week, we invited our regular Satsang community to join for tea and scones, followed by a walk of the land with our Farm Co-Lead Paz, an asana class with our very own Haripriya, and live music with our long time karma yogi, Dani! The evening ended off with a community dinner and apple cider! Thank you for being a part of our community. Jai Satsang!
