We hear this often: *"I'd love to come, but I'm not sure I'm experienced enough."*

We want to set something straight.

The [Yoga Study & Service Immersion](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/yoga-study-and-service-immersion-program/) is not a program for advanced practitioners. It is not a performance or a teacher training. It is for anyone who feels genuinely called to explore what yoga actually is — beyond postures, beyond studios, beyond the version of it that fills social media.

People come to [YSSI](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/yoga-study-and-service-immersion-program/) fresh out of school. People come after decades of working life. People come from Denmark, from Australia, from Japan, from right here in Canada. Some have a daily practice. Some have never rolled out a mat.

What everyone shares is this: a quiet readiness to go deeper. To ask bigger questions. To live, just for one month, inside a rhythm that is slower, more intentional, more honest.

**What those four weeks actually look like:**

Morning sādhana , pranayama, meditation, asana — to begin each day from a grounded place.

Study of yoga philosophy with teachers who have walked this path for decades. Real conversations about what it means to live with integrity, contentment, and care.

Fifteen hours each week of Karma Yoga — meaningful work in the kitchen, the farm, the garden. You will discover that chopping vegetables with full attention is not so different from meditation.

Shared meals. Kirtan. Evening satsang. Laughter. The particular warmth of a community that is trying, genuinely, to be kind.

And around all of it: 69 acres of old cedar forest, orchards, meadows, and the quiet of Salt Spring Island.

You belong here if you are curious. That is enough.
