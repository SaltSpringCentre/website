## *A TEACHING ON FAITH (SRADDHA)*

# *Faith is the ground for yoga to grow.*

# *— Baba Hari Dass*

"A tree is inside a seed in a subtle form. When the seed is sown, the tree comes out in the gross form. In the same way all knowledge is already in our mind and by doing yoga this knowledge comes out like a tree from a seed. Just as rich soil in the ground is important for a seed to grow, faith is the ground for yoga to grow. Faith is the real teacher. Faith is real yoga, and faith is real attainment."

"Wish you happy and success"

- Babaji

Patanjali names faith first among the qualities that carry a practitioner forward. Sraddhā, the ground a practice is sown into before anything else can take root. Not faith as belief in something unseen, but as the willingness to begin, to keep showing up before the result is visible. This is the faith Babaji pointed to again and again, not a feeling, but a practice, renewed daily, the way roots are renewed by water.

Faith is, fittingly, this year's theme, for our[ ](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/)**[Annual Community Yoga Retreat](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/), **now in its 52nd year. Fifty-two years of people joining the Summer Retreats(ACYR) believing in the teachings shared by Baba Hari Dass and his students. It always validates the belief and the circle grows.

We'd love for you to join this tradition this July.

**52ND ANNUAL COMMUNITY YOGA RETREAT — [REGISTRATION IS OPEN](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/)**

**Thursday, July 30 – Monday, August 3, 2026 | Salt Spring Centre of Yoga**

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/75a74a64-2a8f-6d9f-b585-a573f6b7a0e6.jpg)

Five days of practice, connection, and renewal in community - yoga sessions for all levels (and for kids), kirtan, workshops, daily ceremony, and wholesome vegetarian meals shared together. It's a family-friendly gathering, rooted in the teachings of Baba Hari Dass, and open to longtime practitioners and first-timers alike.

A good number of you have already registered, and we're so looking forward to seeing you all. There's still room, for you, for your family, for the friend you've been meaning to bring along. If ACYR has been sitting on your mind, this is a gentle nudge: don't wait too long, as accommodation spots do fill before the date itself.
