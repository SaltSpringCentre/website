# If this has been on your mind

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/93244212-51cc-de00-3cf9-143ace6d0add.jpg)

###

Dear Ones,

Sometimes the thought comes quietly:

"I should go back"

Just to return to a steadier rhythm.

A few days of simple practice.

Of being in a space where not much is asked of you.

When you do not have to figure anything out.

We're a week away from the Yoga and Wellness Weekend on Salt Spring.

We currently have 15 participants confirmed with only a few spaces remaining.

You dont need to be fully ready. You dont need to have everything figured out.

If this has been on your mind, you can trust that.

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/252ee6ea-61bf-ffd7-92fe-8fae85f65050.jpg)

[Reserve Your Spot](https://saltspringcentre.com/yoga-and-wellness-weekend-retreats/)

Looking forward to welcoming you!

**With peace and love, 🙏 🕉️ 💖**

**The Salt Spring Centre of Yoga Team**
