A few spots in the [Yoga Study & Service Immersion](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/yoga-study-and-service-immersion-program/) are still open.

If this program has been living somewhere quietly in the back of your mind, if you've read our emails and felt something stir, but haven't quite taken the step

we want to speak to that part of you directly.

This is a month to **live** inside a rhythm that is slower and more intentional than ordinary life. To **learn** from Sharada Filkow, Anuradha Hannah, Yogeshwar Humphrey and other long-time practitioners who have devoted their lives to the teachings of Baba Hari Dass, teachings rarely found in modern yoga spaces. To **serve** with your hands in the kitchen, the garden, the land and discover that this, too, is practice. To **grow** in ways that are quiet, real, and lasting.

## Babaji said: *"My only aim is to bring people in sadhana."*

Not to perform yoga. Not to achieve something. Simply to practice and to be held while you do.

That is what these four weeks offer. A container. A community. A genuine return to yourself.

The application deadline is **June 15**. The program begins **July 3**.

[Apply Now!](https://saltspringcentre.secure.retreat.guru/program/residential-yoga-study-and-service-immersion/?form=1&lang=en)

##

Don’t miss your chance to embark on this life-changing journey.

This is the only Yoga Study & Service Immersion Program this year, and spots are limited!

P.S. Applications are reviewed through an interview process. Secure your spot today and start your path to transformation!

If you have any questions or need more information, feel free to contact us at info@saltspringcentre.com, and we’ll be happy to assist.
