### Day Passes & Meal Tickets

If you are planning to join us for one day or commuter at the Retreat, [D](https://saltspringcentre.com/annual-community-yoga-retreat/acyr-meal-registration/)[ay and Commuter ](https://saltspringcentre.secure.retreat.guru/program/annual-community-yoga-retreat-2026-day-pass-meals-classes/?form=1&lang=en)passes & meal tickets are now available for pre-purchase online.

**Want to join us for all of our family-friendly retreat activities? **See the website for [more registration options!](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/)

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/98780ef7-8eb5-51ad-1595-9d6daab76f8a.jpg)

Last year, your generosity allowed** four families **with financial challenges to attend the Annual Retreat.

If you would like to help support individuals with financial barriers to attend ACYR, you have the option to add a donation to your registration. Simply look for the ***Sponsor a Low-Income Attendee*** box on your retreat registration form to enter your contribution!

*If you missed the opportunity to donate when you registered, you can send a donation via e-transfer (payment@saltspringcentre.com). Please indicate "Annual Retreat Donation" in the memo field.*

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/c966bc24-de92-971c-2e41-f954ea3c8712.jpg)

### Aparigraha Sale!

Bring your treasures to sell at the Aparigraha Sale. An opportunity to declutter your mind - and closet. A tradition at retreat, we invite you to bring clothing, jewlery, and other items as donations to the Centre.
