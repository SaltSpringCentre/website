Dear Friends,

Registration for this year’s [**Annual Community Yoga Retreat** ](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/)will be closing in 2 days on July 25, and I wanted to send one final invitation.

We would really love to see you.

Each year, ACYR is a chance for our yoga family to come together. Friends reunite, new people join us, and our circle continues to grow. Every person who comes is part of what makes this gathering so meaningful.

If you’ve been thinking about coming, I hope you’ll join us.

We've turned a triple into a double room, so there is still space for you. Email the [office ](mailto:info@saltspringcentre.com)to confirm your booking.

The [retreat schedule ](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/)is now available, and we’re looking forward to a beautiful week of yoga, meditation, pranayama, satsang, karma yoga, kirtan, children’s activities, inspiring guest speakers, and simply spending time together in community.

For our Salt Spring Island friends, you’re warmly invited to drop in for Satsang, Aarati, and other community gatherings throughout the retreat. These are open to everyone, and we’d love to welcome you.

Thank you for being part of this community. Whether you’ve been coming for many years or are thinking about joining us for the first time, you are welcome here.

I hope to see you soon.

With love,

**Anuradha**

Salt Spring Centre of Yoga

## [**ANNUAL COMMUNITY YOGA RETREAT**](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/annual-community-yoga-retreat/)

**July 30 - August 3 **

For over fifty years, this retreat has been the heartbeat of our summer, the moment when alumni, longtime Satsang, and first-time guests all find themselves on the same land, in the same practice, at the same table. This year marks the 52nd, and the invitation is the same as it has always been: come deepen your practice, be with your people, and let the land do what it does.

Daily asana, pranayama, and meditation for all levels, kirtan and ceremony rooted in the teachings of Baba Hari Dass. Workshops, community gatherings, and wholesome vegetarian meals, with programming for kids and families throughout.

## [**GURU PURNIMA**](https://saltspringcentre.com/events/guru-purnima-celebration-2026/)

**Tuesday, July 28

8:30am–12:00pm,

lunch by donation to follow**

Two days before the ACYR retreat begins, we pause to honour Babaji and all spiritual teachers through the ancient Vedic yajna, a quiet threshold before the fuller gathering that follows.

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/images/7d636ec8-f9b4-dc30-e69b-8337b1e323c3.jpg)

The morning opens with an invocation to Ganesh, moves through repetitions of the Hanuman Chalisa, and closes with the Guru Purnima yajna itself, prayers, mantra, and offerings from the heart. Lunch follows by donation; please RSVP to the office. Interested in offering at the yajna, or staying the night before? Contact Mahavir or the office.
