## Reclaim your direction. Reset your mindset. Realign your life.

### 📆 September 10-14, 2026

[Register Now!](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/more-from-life-retreat/)

If you have been feeling the pull to go even deeper into your yoga studies, we have a new offering this Fall, just for you. An experiential retreat where you focus on purifying the mind through letting go of patterns, beliefs and energetic blocks on the path to fulfilling your dharma and fully loving yourself.

“Love everyone, including yourself, that is real sadhana” ~ Babaji

There are moments when you begin to realize you can keep yourself busy, distracted, moving from one thing to the next, and still feel something unresolved underneath. You have “done the work”; read the books and listened to the podcasts. You have been seeking and learning but…the same patterns keep returning. Something is holding you back in how you live, love, and show up.

This is because “the work” is not about the learning, and the moments of calm and even bliss, it is about the deep body and mind remembering, integration, and recalibration.

There comes a point where you can no longer escape it. Something deeper calls you to stop avoiding and turn inward.

Because understanding your patterns is not the same as being free from them. Because real change doesn’t happen at the surface. It happens in the emotional and energetic layers of who you are, where safety, self-worth, and acceptance are actually felt.

We invite you to take a look and see if this retreat is exactly what you have been seeking.
