# More From Life Retreat

## Reclaim your direction. Reset your mindset. Realign your life.

### 📆 September 10-14, 2026

[Register Now!](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/more-from-life-retreat/)

I did all the things I thought I was supposed to do. I got a good job and started to walk down the path of having a home and a family, but everything felt wrong. I didn't understand back then that all of the wounds I was carrying led to the life that I had manifested. I was where I was and feeling stuck because of things that didn't even belong to me in the first place.

I started the journey with a yoga teacher training at the center, then the Ayurveda training at Mount Madonna, then a deep dive into spiritual books, podcasts, philosophy, psychology, energetics, and manifestation. I dove deep into childhood trauma, mirroring, sabotage patterns, and the psychology of beliefs.

I analyzed myself, every relationship, and every client I worked with.

But this didn't change my life. It still looked the same. Although I could explain it all and host a webinar, I was missing the feeling, the internal transformation, the energetic switch, the action from this more authentic place. I was still playing it safe even though I completely thought I wasn't.

And that's why this retreat is different. You'll not only get the highlights of all of these systems, but what we're going to focus on is the physical release of the things that have been holding you back. A deep purification of the mind. This is going to be an opportunity to feel, and to let go. This is about action, embodiment, and leaving lighter and more you.

If you've read this far into the email, you have some interest in attending. So let's talk about what happens if you don't: life goes on the same, and you continue to do the work on your own, slowly getting to the place you want to be.

If you come, you'll be in a container with other people doing the same work. And when folks come together, it multiplies the effects of the work tenfold. You'll have support, insights, and you won't have to do it alone. This will move you forward, and you'll make some good friends along the way.

Now it's time for you to decide.

Either way, we love you and wish you all the success on your journey.

## Why join this retreat?

Because you’re ready.

Because it’s time to come back to **you**.
