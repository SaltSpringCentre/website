Let us paint you a picture.

It is just after sunrise. The light is that particular gold that only exists in early summer on Salt Spring Island. The cedars are still. A sparrow calls once from somewhere in the orchard, then quiet again.

In the Satsang Room, fifteen people settle onto their mats and cushions. Someone rings a bell.

The breath begins.

For the next hour and a half, there is nothing to do but this. Breathe. Move. Sit. Be still. No phone. No to-do list pressing at the edges of your mind. Just the room, the light coming through the windows, the sound of breath and, later, of voices chanting together.

Then breakfast made by hand in the Centre kitchen, organic, nourishing, eaten together around circular tables. Conversation that goes somewhere real.

The day unfolds from there. Study. Service. Free time to walk the land or sit by the water. Evening gathering. Rest.

Babaji said it simply:

## *"If you are in the present moment you are not in the past or future. All peace is in the present moment."*

This is what a month at the [YSSI](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/yoga-study-and-service-immersion-program/) feels like. Not dramatic. Not performative. Just a steady, held, honest rhythm.
