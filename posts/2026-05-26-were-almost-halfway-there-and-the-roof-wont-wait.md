Thank you so very much for the support for the new roof for the school!! It takes a village and everyone, every donation, every share is taking us there!!

Cut from old-growth Douglas Fir and raised as a school in a logging town, these timbers were saved from demolition and carried home to Blackburn Road. They are serving children again. The [Salt Spring Nature Academy](https://saltspringnatureacademy.com/) welcomed 24 children this year, with 40+ already registered for the fall.
