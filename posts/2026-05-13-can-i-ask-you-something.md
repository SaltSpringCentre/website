Namaste Friend,

You've read about the [Yoga Study & Service Immersion](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/yoga-study-and-service-immersion-program/). Something brought you back to look again.

I want to ask, gently: what's getting in the way?

For most people it's one of three things:

The timing. A month feels like a long time to step away.

The cost. $1,600 is not a small investment.

The unknown. An interview process, a new community, a different way of living, even for four weeks.

All of that is real. And all of it is worth sitting with.

What we can tell you is this: the people who have come before you felt the same things. And almost universally, what they say afterward is that the only thing they wish they'd done differently is come sooner.

"The opportunity to live a life in spiritual community has been deeply meaningful."

— Joe, past YSSI participant

"The teachings of Babaji deepened my Karma Yoga experience. I was able to relax and enjoy each moment."

— Tana, past YSSI participant

The [application deadline](https://saltspringcentre.com/programs-retreats/yoga-study-and-service-immersion-program/) is June 15. The program begins June 29. There are very few spots left.

If something in you is still leaning toward this, the next step is simply to apply. The application is the conversation, not a commitment.

And if you have a question you'd like to ask before you do, reply to this email. I'll answer personally.

With gratitude,

Ranju Raman

Salt Spring Centre of Yoga
