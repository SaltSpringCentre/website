# On the search for peace in daily life

[

![](https://mcusercontent.com/723efab91f9f92d89520cd8d7/video_thumbnails_new/f4853aceab6f1d2f6308138b76d9a71f.png)
](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gdaAtkhmjH8)

In this 5-minute reflection, Anuradha, longtime student of Baba Hari Dass, shares a teaching from Babaji that has deeply influenced the way she moves through daily life, moment by moment. Drawing from his essay Binding Thoughts and Liberation, she offers a simple practice: when something arises, pause and ask yourself: Is this binding me to the drama and suffering, or is it leading me toward cultivating peace and well-being? Choose peace. It is a lifetime practice.

[WATCH THE VIDEO](http://youtube.com/watch?v=gdaAtkhmjH8&feature=youtu.be)
