Our Living Tradition

50+ Years. One Community.

Every summer for more than half a century, the Annual Community Yoga Retreat has called practitioners home to the Centre. What began as a small gathering of seekers in the 1970s has grown into a multi-generational reunion: grandparents who first came as students, now sharing kirtan with grandchildren who run barefoot across the same fields.

Rooted in the teachings of Baba Hari Dass and the spirit of seva, the ACYR is a living, breathing tradition. Diverse yoga styles, sacred music, shared vegetarian meals, dedicated programming for children and teens, and the quiet companionship of 69 acres of forest, garden, and ocean.

What You'll Find Here

Six threads woven into every retreat.

Diverse Yoga Styles

Hatha, gentle, restorative, vinyasa, yin, chair, and more. Classes for every body and every level.

Kirtan & Sacred Music

Evenings of call-and-response chanting, harmonium, and tabla.

Workshops & Teachings

Philosophy, pranayama, sacred ceremony, and conversations with senior teachers.

Children & Teens Programming

Dedicated kids' camp and teen programming, run in parallel with adult sessions.

Communal Vegetarian Meals

Three nourishing meals a day, much of it grown in the gardens.

69 Acres of Sacred Land

Forested trails, gardens, the Madonna grove, the swimming pond, and ocean vistas.

Authentic Yoga Lineage

Deepen Your Practice

The retreat draws on a rich lineage of classical yoga, with teachings rooted in the tradition of Baba Hari Dass. Expect kirtan, sacred ceremony, pranayama, philosophy, meditation, and asana led by experienced teachers.

All Ages, All Levels

A Family Reunion

Multi-generational by design. Grandparents, parents, kids, and teens each have their own programming, and plenty of shared moments in between. Children's camp runs in parallel with adult sessions. Come as you are.
