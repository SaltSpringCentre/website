Our Living Tradition

50+ Years. One Community.

Every summer for more than half a century, the Annual Community Yoga Retreat has called practitioners home to the Centre. What began as a small gathering of seekers in the 1970s has grown into a multi-generational reunion: grandparents who first came as students, now sharing kirtan with grandchildren who run barefoot across the same fields.

Rooted in the teachings of Baba Hari Dass and the spirit of seva, the ACYR is a living, breathing tradition. Diverse yoga styles, sacred music, shared vegetarian meals, dedicated programming for children and teens, and the quiet companionship of 69 acres of forest, garden, and ocean. The Centre's flagship event, and for many, the heart of the year.

What You'll Find Here

Six threads woven into every retreat.

Diverse Yoga Styles

Hatha, gentle, restorative, vinyasa, yin, chair, and more. Classes for every body and every level, from sunrise practice through evening wind-down.

Kirtan & Sacred Music

Evenings of call-and-response chanting, harmonium, and tabla. The kind of singing that loosens the chest and lifts a whole hall together.

Workshops & Teachings

Philosophy, pranayama, sacred ceremony, creative expression, and conversations with senior teachers in the Baba Hari Dass lineage.

Children & Teens Programming

Dedicated kids' camp and teen programming run in parallel with adult sessions. Crafts, games, story time, nature walks, and age-appropriate yoga.

Communal Vegetarian Meals

Three nourishing meals a day prepared by the Centre's kitchen, much of it grown in the gardens just outside the dining hall.

69 Acres of Sacred Land

Forested trails, gardens, the Madonna grove, the swimming pond, and ocean vistas. Space to wander, sit, and be quiet between sessions.

Authentic Yoga Lineage

Deepen Your Practice

The retreat draws on a rich lineage of classical yoga, with teachings rooted in the tradition of Baba Hari Dass. Expect kirtan, sacred ceremony, pranayama, philosophy, meditation, and asana led by experienced teachers.

From sunrise sadhana to evening kirtan, every moment is an invitation to grow and connect.

All Ages, All Levels

A Family Reunion

Multi-generational by design. Grandparents, parents, kids, and teens each have their own programming, and plenty of shared moments in between. Children's camp runs in parallel with adult sessions, with crafts, games, story time, and age-appropriate yoga.

Come as you are. Everyone is welcome.

Work honestly, meditate every day, meet people without fear, and play.
Baba Hari Dass
